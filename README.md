
# 🍭 Candy Shop Math Quiz

### ✨ Knowledge Is Money — Learn Math, Earn Candy!

A colorful, story-driven educational browser game built using **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**.


## 📖 The Story

A little boy walks into a candy shop, excited to buy his favorite candy. Unfortunately, he has no money!

The friendly shopkeeper makes him a special offer:

> 💡 **"Knowledge is money! Answer math questions and earn candy!"**

The boy accepts the challenge and starts his mathematical adventure.


## 🎮 Game Journey

### 🏪 1. Enter the Candy Shop


The boy enters the colorful candy shop using a CSS sprite walking animation.

### 🚶 2. Meet Our Little Hero



The boy interacts with the shopkeeper through animated scenes, speech bubbles, and voice recordings.

### 🧠 3. Think and Solve



Random math questions appear while the boy thinks and responds.

### 🍬 4. Earn Your Candy


The score increases as the game progresses. Once the boy collects **5 points**, he wins a giant cotton candy!

### 🏆 5. Victory Celebration


Victory music plays, the candy reward appears, and the game transitions to its completion screen.
